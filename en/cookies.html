3. What "similar technologies" are

Besides cookies, there are identifiers and other mechanisms that store or read information on your device, for example the Android advertising ID (a random code you can reset or delete). European and other laws (such as Directive 2002/58/EC, art. 5(3)) require your consent to store or read information on your device unless it is strictly necessary to provide a service you asked for. In Chile, Law 19,628 applies and, from 1 December 2026, Law 21,719, which require clear information and free, informed consent for personalised advertising.

The site with the legal texts is static: it sets no cookies or identifiers in your browser and has no analytics, fonts or third-party scripts. Like any web server, the hosting provider (GitHub, Inc., United States, through GitHub Pages) may log your IP address, browser type and visit time in its logs, for security and operation. We do not have access to those logs and do not use them. See GitHub's privacy statement for more information.

6. How to change or withdraw your consent

  • In the app: Settings → Privacy and ads → Manage options. Google's form opens, where you can change or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what happened before.
  • On Android: Settings → Google (or Security and privacy) → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. The app's ads are already non-personalised; this Android setting also affects other apps.
  • Remove ads: subscribe to Premium (managed in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions).
  • Residents of California and other US states: you may use your opt-out choices (although we do not run personalised advertising) from the same privacy options menu, where Google shows it for your region, or by writing to us.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the app's local data from your phone.

This does not affect system permissions (camera, microphone, approximate location, notifications), which you can withdraw in Android settings.
