Boulder Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Version 1.0 · pending final legal review.

1. Who we are

Boulder Tracker (the "app") lets you record your bouldering climbs on video, organize them by gym and grade, and track your progress. It is published by Cristian Felipe Castro Ponce (BClabs), located at Villarrica, Chile ("we"). Contact for anything about your data: vulcanoedicionessa@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • Your videos, notes, gyms and stats are stored on your phone. Boulder Tracker has no servers of its own and no accounts of its own.
  • If you turn it on, you can back up your videos to your own Google Drive.
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Ads never see your videos, notes or gyms.
  • Premium (no ads, cloud backup) is paid through Google Play and managed with RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell your data.

3. Data we process

3.1 Data that stays on your device

Videos and thumbnails, grades, attempts, notes, gyms you add (name, address, contact, social links), your profile (name, location, bio and avatar if you fill them in), settings and achievements. It does not leave your phone except as explained in 3.2 to 3.5.

3.2 Device permissions

  • Camera and microphone: to record videos. Requested when you use the recorder.
  • System photo and video picker: to import videos or pick an image. The app has no access to your gallery: it only receives what you choose.
  • Approximate location: only when you tap "detect location" while creating a gym, to fill in country, region and city. Coordinates are sent to the Android/Google geocoding service to turn them into a place name and are not stored on our systems.
  • Notifications: local training reminders, if you enable them. We do not use remote notifications.

3.3 Google Drive (optional)

If you connect your Google account, the app can upload your videos and a data file to a "Boulder Tracker" folder in your Drive, and download them back. It only uses the drive.file scope: the app can only see files it creates itself. We receive your email address to show you which account is connected. The files stay in your Drive; we do not see or store them. You can disconnect in Settings and delete the folder from Drive at any time.

Boulder Tracker's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3.4 Advertising (free version)

We use Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device data, approximate location derived from your IP, ad interaction data and diagnostics, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Depending on your country we ask for consent through Google's form (UMP); without consent only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy and ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or remove ads with Premium. More: How Google uses data.

3.5 Premium and purchases

Subscriptions are charged by Google Play; we do not receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate your subscription: it receives an anonymous identifier generated for you, purchase information (product, dates, status), basic device data and your IP address. We do not send it your email, name, videos or notes. More: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Who we share it with

  • Google (AdMob, Google Play, Google Drive and location services), as described.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (subscription validation).
  • Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell personal data. These providers may process data outside your country (for example, in the United States) under the safeguards in their policies.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The app does not use cookies. The advertising and purchase identifiers described above serve a similar purpose and are explained in sections 3.4 and 3.5. This website does not use cookies or analytics tools.

7. Retention

  • Local data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Files in your Drive: until you delete them.
  • Data held by AdMob and RevenueCat: per those providers' policies. If you ask for deletion, we will help you manage it with them.
  • We keep no copies of your data on servers of our own.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you have the right to access, rectify, erase, object, restrict and port your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. This includes Chile (Law 19,628 and, from 1 December 2026, Law 21,719), the EU (GDPR) and California (CCPA). To exercise them, write to vulcanoedicionessa@gmail.com; we reply within legal deadlines. Because most of your data is only on your phone, you can exercise many of these rights directly in the app (edit, export, delete).

How to delete your data: delete videos and gyms in the app; use "Remove from Drive" or delete the "Boulder Tracker" folder in your Drive; disconnect your account in Settings; uninstall the app. For ads and purchase data, manage your advertising ID in Android and write to us so we can coordinate with RevenueCat.

9. Children

Boulder Tracker is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child gave us data, write to us and we will delete it. In some countries the minimum age of consent is higher (up to 16).

10. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Communications with Google, AdMob and RevenueCat use encrypted connections. No system is perfect; protect your phone and your Google account.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will publish the new version here with its date and, for important changes, notify you in the app.
