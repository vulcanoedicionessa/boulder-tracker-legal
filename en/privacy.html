Boulder Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 October 2026 · Version: 2.1 (minimum age 14 and always non-personalized ads; replaces v2.0 of 4 October 2026)

1. Summary

  • Your videos, notes, gyms and stats are stored only on your phone. Boulder Tracker has no servers of its own, no accounts of its own and no analytics of its own.
  • If you turn it on, you can back up your data to your own Google Drive (a Premium feature).
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads, always non-personalized (you are not profiled for advertising), and only if you allow it when Google asks (where the law requires it). Ads never see your videos, notes or gyms.
  • Premium (no ads, cloud backup) is paid through Google Play and validated with RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell your data for money. You can delete it whenever you want.
  • Boulder Tracker is for people aged 14 or over. If you are 14-17, read section 11.

2. Who is responsible

The data controller is Cristian Felipe Castro Ponce, a natural person, trading as BClabs, based in Villarrica, Chile ("we"), who publishes the Boulder Tracker app on Google Play.

Privacy and rights contact: vulcanoedicionessa@gmail.com (subject "Privacy"). This same address is the channel for data protection queries in every country (including the LGPD "encarregado" in Brazil).

3. Data we process

3.1 Data that stays on your phone

Videos and thumbnails, grades, attempts, notes, gyms you add (name, address, coordinates, phone, website and social links), your profile (name, location, bio and avatar, if you fill them in), settings and achievements. They are in the app's private storage and we do not receive them. If you record other people or include their data, you are responsible for respecting their privacy and for what you share or export.

If you use "Export backup" or share a video, the file leaves your phone to wherever you send it; that choice is yours.

3.2 Phone permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only to record videos (with audio) inside the app.
  • System photo and video picker: to import videos or pick an image. The app has no access to your gallery, only to what you choose.
  • Approximate location: only when you tap "detect location" while creating a gym. The app reads your approximate location once and converts it into country, region, city and address using the Android/Google system geocoding service, which may receive the coordinates. The coordinates are stored on your phone, in the gym's record, and go to your Drive if you turn on backup. We do not receive them. If you tap "View on map", Google Maps opens with that address or those coordinates.
  • Notifications: local training reminders, if you turn them on. We do not use remote notifications.
  • Internet and network state: for Drive, ads and Premium.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID permission): declared by the ads library; see section 3.5.

3.3 Gym catalog

The app includes a catalog of climbing gyms: Chile (our own catalog from public information) and other countries with OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL license). It is business data (name, city, address, website, Instagram and, for a commercial landline, phone). We do not include gym emails or mobile phone numbers in the Chile catalog. The data ships inside the app and nothing of yours is sent to obtain it. It may be incomplete or out of date. If you are a gym and want to correct or remove your data, write to us.

3.4 Google Drive backup (optional, Premium)

If you connect your Google account, the app receives your email address (only to show which account is connected) and can upload and download files in a "Boulder Tracker" folder in your Drive: your videos and a file with the app's data (profile, notes, gyms with their coordinates, and stats). It uses only the drive.file permission: the app can only see files it creates itself. The files stay in your Drive; we do not see or store them.

Google Limited Use. Boulder Tracker's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: we use Drive data only to provide the backup and restore feature you see in the app; we do not transfer it to third parties (except to comply with law, for security, or with your consent); we do not use it for advertising; no humans read it (except with your explicit consent, for security, or by law); and we do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

3.5 Advertising (free version)

We use Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect the advertising ID, your IP address, device data, approximate location derived from your IP, information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Ads are always non-personalized: the app tells Google to treat every user as under the age of consent and to serve only non-personalized ads, so your interests or history are not used to pick them (they rely on context, such as the app's content, and on frequency caps). Google acts as an independent controller of that data (how Google uses data). We never send it your videos, notes, gyms or email. - Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and where other laws require it (for example some US states), Google shows its consent form (UMP) before ads are requested. If there is no usable consent, the app does not request ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and ads. - You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings, or remove ads with Premium (with Premium the app does not start the ads SDK). - Non-personalized ads for everyone. Because the app admits users from age 14 and cannot verify age, we set all ads to non-personalized (we flag the app as "treat users as under the age of consent"), request only non-personalized ads and limit content to a PG rating. That is not the same as zero data: Google may still process the advertising ID, IP and device data to deliver and measure ads, cap frequency, prevent fraud and comply with the law, under its own responsibility. We build no advertising profiles. With Premium, the app does not start the ads SDK. - We do not flag the app as "child-directed" (it is not: the minimum age is 14).

3.6 Premium and purchases

Subscriptions are charged through Google Play: we do not receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc., a provider acting on our behalf, to validate your subscription. It receives an anonymous identifier generated for you (we do not link it to your email or Google account), purchase information (product, dates, status), basic device data and your IP address. We do not send it your email, name, videos or notes. More information: RevenueCat privacy policy. You cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions; uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

3.7 What we do not do

We do not use our own analytics, do not profile you or make automated decisions with legal effects about you, do not knowingly process sensitive data, and do not use your data to train AI. The ads we show are non-personalized: there is no advertising profiling based on your data.

We reply without undue delay and generally within 30 calendar days (or any shorter period set by law, for example 15 days in Brazil), extendable where the law allows. How to delete your data 1. In the app: delete videos, gyms and notes, and edit or empty your profile. 2. Purchases: email us to request deletion of your anonymous ID and we will coordinate with RevenueCat; cancel the subscription in Google Play.

Minimum age. Boulder Tracker is for people aged 14 or over and is not directed at anyone under 14. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14. The app does not verify age: your age is a statement you make. If you believe a child under 14 has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

If you are 14-17. - You may use the free app. Almost all your data stays only on your phone and we do not receive it. - The ads you see are always non-personalized (section 3.5). We do not sell your data or use it for targeted advertising. - Premium: if you are under 18, you may buy it only with the authorization of your parent or legal guardian, or if an adult buys it with their own Google Play account, as Google Play's terms and consumer law require. Drive backup requires a Google account that meets Google's own age requirements. - We recommend talking to a parent or guardian before connecting Drive, using location, or recording other people.

Parents and guardians. Write to vulcanoedicionessa@gmail.com (subject "Privacy: minor") to ask about, correct or request deletion of a minor's data we hold (for example, their message to us or their anonymous RevenueCat purchase ID); we will coordinate deletion with RevenueCat. We may ask for minimal information to verify the relationship. Data on the minor's phone (videos, notes, profile) can be deleted from the app or Android settings (section 8); Drive files are managed by the minor's Google account.

What the laws say (summary). - Chile (Law 19.628, Law 21.719 and Law 21.430): children under 14 need their parents' or representatives' consent; teenagers 14-17 can generally consent themselves, except for certain sensitive data, and the child's best interests and progressive autonomy guide all processing. We do not knowingly process sensitive data. - EU/EEA: GDPR art. 8 lets each country set the age of consent between 13 and 16 for processing based on consent (for example, 14 in Spain and Italy, 15 in France and 16 in Germany). The app is for people aged 14 or older and all ads are non-personalized; if the age of consent in your country is higher than yours, you need the authorization of your parent or legal guardian: write to us and we will handle the request. United Kingdom: the Children's Code applies to services minors are likely to use; for now the app is not offered there. - Brazil: the LGPD (art. 14) and the ECA Digital (Law 15.211/2025) require protecting the best interests of children and teenagers; we use non-personalized ads and avoid collecting unnecessary data. - US: COPPA protects under-13s, who are outside the app; some state laws protect teenagers (for example, limiting targeted advertising), which is why we do not do targeted advertising.

If a breach affecting you occurs and the law requires it, we will notify you and the authority within the legal deadlines. To report a vulnerability: vulcanoedicionessa@gmail.com.
